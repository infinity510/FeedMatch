🧠 The Mathematics Behind It

  • 📈

    Bipartite Graph: People and food items form two disjoint sets. Preferences are the edges.

  • 🎯

    Maximum Matching: Trying to maximize valid assignments.

  • ⚡

    Augmenting Path (Rescue): A chain of reassignments that increases the total matched pairs.

  • 🛑

    Hall's Marriage Theorem: A perfect match is impossible if any subgroup of N people share fewer than N food options (as seen in Round 3B).
